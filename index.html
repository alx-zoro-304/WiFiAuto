Capabilities

Engineered to do the impossible

Every feature runs inside one script — no installs, no dependencies, no external binaries.

📡

Open Network Finder

Scans and lists every open (passwordless) WiFi network around you and connects with a single click.

🔍

Deep Device Scanner

Sweeps 2044 addresses across /22 + /24 scopes in repeated 15-second rounds, merging every ARP sighting until even quiet devices appear.

⚡

Activity Sniffer & Ranking

Ranks device traffic and always tries the busiest MAC first — smart order, not random guesses.

🛡️

Native MAC Spoofing

Disable → registry write → enable, proven on Realtek 8821CE. If the driver reverts, the tool skips it instantly and moves on.

🌐

Auto Internet Recovery

After every MAC change it tests real connectivity and repeats until the internet works — or you press Stop.

🧩

Self-Contained & Portable

One file, zero dependencies. Native Python pings, inline PowerShell, no .exe helpers, no .ps1 scripts. Works on Windows & Linux.

The flow

How it works

Four stages. Zero supervision needed after you press Start.

01

Find & connect

Scans open networks, you pick one, it connects instantly.

02

Deep scan

Sweeps thousands of addresses in rounds and collects every device MAC on the network.

03

Sniff & rank

Watches traffic 10 seconds and ranks which devices are most active.

04

Spoof & verify

Adopts the best scanned MAC, reconnects, tests the internet, repeats until success.
